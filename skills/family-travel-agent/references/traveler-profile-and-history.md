# Traveler Profile and Trip History

Reuse only confirmed private preferences. Ask only questions that materially
change the current plan.

## High-impact questions

- Who travels, their role or relationship, and age-at-trip. Never store a birth date.
- Per traveler: allergies, dietary practices, avoided ingredients, likes, and
  dislikes. Allergies are safety-critical and never inferred from dislikes.
- Accommodation format, bed/room arrangement, bathroom privacy, accessibility,
  and budget.
- Transport preference, acceptable daily travel time, wake and meal rhythm,
  mobility/rest needs, language support, shopping interests, and sensory needs.

Unknown is valid. Do not turn a trip-specific choice into a permanent family
preference.

## Confirmation and storage

Label every proposed profile change with value, traveler, scope, source or
confirmation date, and confidence. Ask for explicit confirmation before
creating, correcting, or removing a durable preference.

In LLMWiki-enhanced mode, store confirmed values only in the authorized private
Wiki under its repository contract. Preserve raw evidence and unrelated edits.
In standalone mode, return a copyable profile summary and do not silently
persist personal data or claim future memory.

## Trip history

Retain dates and destinations, travelers and age-at-trip, final itinerary,
materially revised versions, accommodation, dining, transport, shopping,
reservation states, open loops, applied preferences, and post-trip lessons.
Do not store every draft or duplicate sensitive evidence.

At the next trip, state which confirmed preferences and lessons are being
reused and ask whether they remain valid. Allow correction, deletion, or
trip-specific scope at any time.
