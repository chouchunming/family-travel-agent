# Shopping and Dining

Treat meals and purchases as route constraints, not a must-visit checklist.

## Select useful stops

1. Collect desired item or meal, dietary needs, budget, available time, route,
   transport, luggage capacity, and whether the request is exact-SKU or
   exploratory.
2. Recommend one to three stops that consolidate needs: a station complex,
   mall, market, outlet, roadside stop, neighborhood cluster, or
   accommodation-adjacent branch.
3. Use the official store or venue locator to verify the actual branch, hours,
   access, visitor entrance, parking/transit, reservation policy, and check
   date. Verify stock separately for an exact item.
4. Explain route fit and keep extra branches as fallbacks.

## Destination checks

Use the destination module or current official guidance for taxes, visitor
refunds, service charges, tipping, cash/card expectations, alcohol or food
rules, opening patterns, and reservation etiquette. Never apply another
country's chain catalog or tax process by analogy.

For an exact SKU, verify model, variant, inventory, pickup window, original
price, returns, warranty region, voltage/frequency/plug, consumables, transport
restrictions, and luggage weight or size. Before delivery to accommodation,
confirm its policy, guest-name format, arrival window, storage, and checkout.

Prefer local specialties that fit the party's dietary and safety needs. Keep
allergies separate from dislikes. Distinguish researched recommendations from
confirmed reservations or inventory holds.

## Output and action gate

For each option state route fit, verified branch facts and date, time allowance,
access, booking or stock state, destination-specific cautions, and one
fallback. Do not order, reserve, pay, or message without explicit approval.
