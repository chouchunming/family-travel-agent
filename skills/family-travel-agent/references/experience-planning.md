# Experience Planning

Use this workflow for tickets, tours, lessons, seasonal programs, and other
bookable experiences.

## Define the need

Record destination, date window, party and age-at-trip, accessibility or
language needs, fixed reservations, route, transport, desired intensity,
weather tolerance, and budget. Unknown restrictions remain open loops.

## Find and compare

Start with the official operator, then compare relevant established booking
channels such as Agoda, KKday, Klook, or destination-specific platforms.
Shortlist one to three options. Verify the actual product, operator, meeting
point, duration, language, age/height/health limits, inclusions, exclusions,
transport, meals, family fit, route efficiency, cancellation, weather policy,
availability, and check date.

Compare original-currency total party price after mandatory taxes and fees.
If a common-currency estimate helps, label it approximate and record the
exchange-rate source and check date. Prefer fit and booking confidence over a
headline discount.

## Preserve state

A listing is a candidate. Payment or a request receipt is not confirmation.
Use the reservation workflow states and mark unresolved conflict or stale
availability `needs-verification`. Draft and compare freely; obtain explicit
approval before sending, submitting, cancelling, or paying.
