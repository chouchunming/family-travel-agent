# Destination Module Index

Load one module for each supported destination. A module supplements the
neutral core; it does not replace evidence, privacy, reservation-state, or
external-action rules.

## Unsupported-destination discovery

If no module exists, do not borrow another destination's conventions. Identify
every destination and border crossing, then check current primary official
sources for:

- entry, passport/visa, customs, and health requirements;
- local driving credentials, road law, rentals, tolls, parking, and navigation;
- rail, transit, ferry, aviation, and accessibility;
- public holidays, school breaks, seasonal closures, and major events;
- taxes, visitor refunds, service charges, tipping, payments, and cash;
- safety alerts, weather, natural hazards, insurance, and emergency contacts;
- accommodation, dining, ticketing, reservation, and cancellation conventions;
- language, naming, address, telephone, and accessibility practices.

For each changeable fact, record source, verification date, destination scope,
and confidence. Preserve conflicts and unknowns as open loops. A missing module
is not permission to guess. Propose a reusable module only when the findings
are durable and destination-specific.

For a multi-country trip, keep rules scoped per border segment. Never carry a
driving document, tax rule, holiday, payment convention, or booking assumption
across a border without separate verification.
