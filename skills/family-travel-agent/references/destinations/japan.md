# Japan Destination Module

Apply this module only to Japan segments. Verify changeable requirements and
operating information against current official sources for the travel dates.

## Calendar and demand periods

Check the Cabinet Office calendar for Japanese public holidays, substitute
holidays, and holidays created between two public holidays. Separately check
weekends, local school holidays, festivals, venue notices, and demand periods
such as Golden Week, Obon, and New Year. Do not call a demand period a national
holiday unless it is legally listed.

Assess rail and flight inventory, highway congestion, lodging price or minimum
stays, restaurant closures, attraction hours, and booking deadlines. Record
source and verification date and add a lower-risk date or buffer when needed.

## Driving documents and rental handoff

Verify the traveler's license jurisdiction, Japan-valid driving credential or
translation, passport requirements, rental-company rules, named drivers,
insurance, child seats, vehicle fit, and pickup/return evidence from current
official sources. Do not infer eligibility from another country or an old trip.

For self-drive segments, check ETC card availability, expressway tolls,
fuel/charging, parking height and entrance, narrow roads, seasonal closures,
and the exact rental return route. Preserve Japanese address and telephone
number fallbacks for vehicle navigation.

## Mapcode navigation

Use Mapcode as parking-specific evidence, never as an approximate place label.
Target the official visitor parking entrance or named lot, hotel/facility
vehicle entrance, official facility point, then a trusted map-directory point.
Never silently substitute a building center, bus stop, scenic point, or
inaccessible road.

Prefer official parking evidence, then an established map provider, then a
labeled secondary source. Record the full Mapcode and `*xx` precision suffix,
target type, telephone number, Japanese address, parking/narrow-road notes,
source, verification date, and confidence: `official`, `map-provider`,
`secondary`, or `unverified`.

Older units may accept only the main digits. Preserve the full code and explain
that fallback. Never estimate a Mapcode. If none is reliable, use telephone,
Japanese address, and map link and state the next verification step.

## Accommodation and bathing

Verify room style and bedding, shared large bath, private bath, in-room bath,
tattoo or age restrictions, meal-plan inclusions, dinner start windows,
check-in deadlines, bathing/accommodation taxes, parking, luggage delivery,
and the guest-name format. Do not infer that breakfast or dinner is included.

## Dining and reservations

Keep Japanese reservation names and readings exactly as confirmed. Retain the
local-script venue name, telephone, address, party, course, arrival deadline,
allergy response, cancellation terms, and parking/entrance instructions.
Draft Japanese or English communication, but require approval before sending.

Use official branch locators and current venue policy. A request, concierge
attempt, calendar entry, card authorization, or management link is not proof
of confirmation.

## Shopping, delivery, and tax-free procedure

Verify the actual branch and route fit. Destination-relevant candidates may
include station complexes, department-store food halls, roadside stations,
service areas, local supermarkets, outlets, and official branches of requested
retailers. For exact items, verify SKU, JAN/model, stock, pickup, warranty,
voltage/frequency/plug, airline restrictions, and luggage impact.

Before Amazon.co.jp or other hotel delivery, obtain hotel approval and verify
guest-name format, arrival window, storage, and checkout timing. Keep order and
payment data in the project's private-data file.

Japan tax-free procedures change. Check the current Japan Tourism Agency and
National Tax Agency guidance plus the store's procedure for the travel date;
record the check date and do not rely on a previous trip's method.
