# Itinerary Workflow

Review or update a family trip day without losing fixed commitments.

## Inputs

Collect date, destination, party and family needs, overnight location, fixed
reservation time, accommodation check-in, opening hours and last admission,
transport time, parking or station access, meals, cancellation deadlines,
mobility and language needs, and holiday or peak-period exposure.

## Destination calendar pass

Use the applicable destination module. Otherwise check current official public
holiday calendars, substitute or observed holidays, school breaks, weekends,
major events, and local demand periods. Do not label a demand period a legal
holiday. Record source and verification date.

Assess transport capacity, road congestion, minimum stays, closures, opening
hours, ticket inventory, and booking or cancellation deadlines. Name the
expected impact and mitigation for every materially affected day.

## Feasibility pass

1. Place reservations, lodging, deadlines, and calendar effects first.
2. Add drive, rail, transit, ferry, parking, station, walking, fuel/charging,
   meals, rest buffer, and child or elder breaks.
3. Check closing and last admission against arrival time.
4. Check route order, border crossings, and the correct visitor entrance.
5. Flag conflicts involving reservations, check-in, meals, closures, excessive
   travel, accessibility, or family recovery.
6. Offer the smallest correction and name what must move.

## Output

Show fixed commitments, flexible stops, transport and buffers, meals,
accommodation, navigation identifiers, unresolved risks, and corrections.
Include a **Holiday / peak-period note** with source, verification date,
expected impact, and mitigation, or state that none was identified in the
checked date range.
